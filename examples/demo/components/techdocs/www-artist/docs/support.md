# Support playbook

## "My numbers look wrong"

Insights lag a day and exclude plays under 30 seconds. Check the artist's
numbers in the internal insights tool before escalating.

## "I can't edit my profile"

Check the account's claim on the artist in the admin tool. Edits need the
editor or admin role on that artist.

## "Two of me" (duplicate artist pages)

Send the two artist ids to the data stewards (team-a, `#artists-help`); they
merge them within two working days.
